God's Own Country: Kerala


Kerala often referred to as "God's Own Country" is a narrow tropical state on India's southwestern Malabar Coast. Bound by the Arabian Sea to the west and the soaring Western Ghats to the east, it is famous for its lush landscapes, abundant rainfall, and rich biodiversity.
